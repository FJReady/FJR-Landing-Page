Last updated: 17 August 2026

These terms apply to coaching sessions booked with First Job Ready. Booking a session means you accept them.

1. Bookings and payment

Full payment is taken upfront at the time of booking, through Stripe via Calendly. Your session is confirmed once payment has gone through.

2. Rescheduling

At least 6 hours' notice is required to reschedule a session.

3. Missed sessions

If you miss a session without giving notice, you get one free reschedule. A second no-show forfeits the session, with no further reschedule and no refund.

4. Minimum age

Sessions are for ages 14 and up. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian to make or approve the booking.

5. Satisfaction guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your session, you can have a full refund. Claim it within 7 days of the session by replying to your booking confirmation email.

6. No job guarantee

Coaching improves interview performance and preparation. It does not guarantee a job offer.

7. In-person sessions

In-person sessions are held at a coworking space in Perth. The address is provided when you book.

First Job Ready holds a current Working with Children Check. The check number is available on request and is not published on this site.

For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian must be contactable by phone for the duration of the session. They are not required to stay on site.

If a client's behaviour during a session is inappropriate or unsafe, First Job Ready may end the session. What happens next — a reschedule, a refund, or neither — is decided at First Job Ready's discretion, case by case.

8. Testimonials

Any feedback shared publicly, on this website or on social media, will only be used with the prior permission of the client or, for anyone under 18, their parent or guardian.
