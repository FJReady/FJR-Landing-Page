Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 August 2026

First Job Ready provides interview coaching in Perth, Western Australia, for people starting their career, changing industries or getting back into work. This notice explains what information we collect when you visit this website, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

This website does not have a contact or enquiry form. We collect nothing from you directly here. The three ways information is collected are set out below.

1. When you visit this page

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use this page — which parts they read, where they click, and where they lose interest. Clarity collects:

  • the pages you view and how long you spend on them
  • clicks, taps, scrolling and mouse movement
  • your approximate location (country and city), worked out from your IP address
  • your device type, browser and operating system
  • a session recording — a replay of how you moved through this page

Clarity is configured with strict masking, which means text on the page is hidden in recordings. We see the shape of how you use the page, not what it says back to you.

Clarity stores small files (cookies and local storage) on your device to recognise a returning visit. It is provided by Microsoft, and the information is processed outside Australia, including in the United States. Microsoft's privacy statement explains what they do with it.

2. Advertising (Meta Pixel)

This page runs ads on Facebook and Instagram, and uses the Meta Pixel to measure how well they work. When you visit this page or click Book Now, the Pixel tells Meta:

  • that this page was visited
  • that a booking button was clicked, and which session it was for

Meta uses this to work out whether our ads are reaching people who go on to book a session, so we can stop showing ads to people who won't benefit and spend the budget better. Meta is a United States company and processes this information outside Australia. Meta's privacy policy explains what they do with it, and you can review or adjust how your data is used for ads in your Meta ad preferences.

The Pixel stores a cookie on your device to do this. Blocking or deleting cookies (see section 7, "Your choices") stops it working — the page functions normally either way.

3. When you book a session

Bookings are handled by Calendly, not by this website. Clicking Book Now takes you to Calendly, where you provide your name, email address and anything else the booking form asks for. That information goes to Calendly first, and we receive it so we can run your session.

Calendly is a United States company and processes information outside Australia. See Calendly's privacy notice.

We do not take payment details on this website.

4. Why we collect it

  • Analytics — to see which parts of the page are working and make it clearer.
  • Booking details — to schedule your session, prepare for it, deliver it, and contact you about it.
  • Advertising — to see whether our Facebook and Instagram ads are reaching people who go on to book, and to spend our ad budget on the people it's actually helping.

We do not sell personal information. We do use the Meta Pixel (described in section 2) to measure and improve our advertising — we do not use your information for any other advertising purpose.

5. Young people and parents

Sessions are open to anyone aged 14 and over. If you are under 18, please talk to a parent or guardian before booking — we ask that a parent or guardian makes or approves the booking.

If you are a parent or guardian and would like to see what information we hold about your child, or would like it deleted, let us know — replying to your booking confirmation email will reach us — and we will action it.

6. How long we keep it

Analytics information is held by Microsoft in line with their own retention policy for Clarity. Booking details are kept for as long as we need them to deliver your session and keep reasonable business records, and then deleted.

7. Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The page works normally without them.
  • Most browsers offer a private or incognito window, which limits what is stored.
  • If you have booked with us, you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — replying to your booking confirmation email will reach us.

8. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, please raise it with us first so we can put it right. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
