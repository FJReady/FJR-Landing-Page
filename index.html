Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The 15-minute chat doesn't cost anything. If I think a coaching session would help, I'll send you the details and pricing afterward — nothing's charged without you seeing it first.

Do I need to be on the call?

Not necessarily — the call can be with your young person, with you, or both, whatever's easiest.

Online or in person?

The free chat is online, by video or phone. If we move ahead with a coaching session, that can be online or in person.

What kind of jobs is this for?

Whether it's their first job, a career change, or getting back into the workforce — if they have an interview coming up, or are getting ready to start applying, this is for them.

What if they don't have an interview booked yet?

That's fine — this works just as well as general preparation before applying as it does before a specific interview.
