What We Coach

  1. 1

    Make a Strong First and Last Impression

    Most think a great first impression comes down to a firm handshake. That matters, but hiring managers care more about your attitude and how you carry yourself walking in. The close counts just as much — they often interview 10–20 candidates in a day, and how you end is what they remember.

  2. 2

    Answering Difficult Questions

    Some interview questions are designed to test how you handle pressure and how self-aware you are. Practicing your answers beforehand makes them far easier to tackle.

  3. 3

    Prepare for the Specific Role

    You don't prepare for one role the same way you prepare for another. We work through what the role and industry you're applying for are actually looking for, so your answers land with the person doing the hiring.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens in the session?

For the 60-minute session: a mock interview, coaching on what to improve, then a second mock interview so you can see the difference. The 20-minute session is a single practice interview with written feedback.

Online or in person?

Both. Online sessions run by video; in-person sessions are held at a coworking space in Perth.

What kind of jobs is this for?

Whether it's your first job, a career change, or getting back into the workforce — if you have an interview coming up, or you're getting ready to start applying, this is for you.

What if I don't have an interview booked yet?

That's fine — this works just as well as general preparation before applying as it does before a specific interview.

What if we need to reschedule or cancel?

At least 6 hours' notice to reschedule. One free reschedule is allowed for a missed session without notice; a second no-show forfeits the session.
